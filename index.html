Who We Are

Wasanii Sanaa is a community-based youth organization founded in Kibera, Nairobi in 2013.

Vision

A vibrant equitable society where every youth voice shapes a just future through art.

Mission

To empower youth and marginalized communities through innovative art-based education and advocacy.
